Autumn in Japan

Red maple leaves, ancient temples and quiet streets turn Japan into one of the world's most magical autumn destinations.

MOMIJI SEASON

When Japan
turns crimson.

Autumn in Japan is celebrated through the tradition of koyo, the viewing of changing leaves. Kyoto is especially beautiful when maple trees surround historic temples, stone paths and peaceful gardens.
